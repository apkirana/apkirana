## 🔬 Research

I am a **PhD candidate at the Faculty of Geo-Information Science and Earth Observation (ITC), University of
Twente**, working on **agentic AI for Earth observation**. My research designs large-language-model agents
that plan, select, and chain geospatial tools so that satellite and aerial imagery analysis can run
end to end autonomously — while remaining auditable and correctable by a domain expert.

Two questions drive the current work: how far can a foundation model be trusted to **write and orchestrate
geospatial analysis itself**, and how do we **measure rigorously when it gets that wrong**. The answers
matter for climate monitoring, environmental degradation, and disaster response, where an unnoticed error
in a pipeline becomes a wrong decision on the ground.
